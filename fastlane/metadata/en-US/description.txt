WaiGoals helps you keep the goals that matter — like "stop working at 7 PM" or "post once a week" — with a single tap a day.

Minimal by design, satisfying to use, and completely private.

WHY WAIGOALS
• One-tap tracking. Open the app, tap your goals, done in seconds.
• Flexible schedules. Daily, specific weekdays (Mon/Wed/Fri), or a number of times per week.
• Forgiving streaks. Your current and best streak, side by side. Only the days you're due count — an unfinished today is grace, not a broken streak.
• See your progress. A clean weekly ring, a contribution-style heatmap, a 12-week trend, and your completion rate.
• Gentle reminders. Pick a time per goal; a quiet nudge keeps you on track.
• Milestones worth celebrating. A little confetti when you reach 7, 30, or 100 days.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Everything lives on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking — just your goals.

DESIGNED FOR TODAY'S iPHONE
Built natively with a clean, modern interface, full Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver support.

Set a goal. Tap it each day. Watch the streak grow.
